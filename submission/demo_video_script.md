# Demo Video Script: Multi-Agent Research & Report Factory

**Duration:** 3 Minutes  
**Target Audience:** Evaluators, Engineers, and Enterprise Research Directors  
**Presenter:** Anushka Tandon  
**Repository:** [github.com/AnushkaTandon26/Multi-Agent-Reseach-and-Report-Factory](https://github.com/AnushkaTandon26/Multi-Agent-Reseach-and-Report-Factory)

---

## Part 1: Problem & System Vision (0:00 – 0:40)
- **Visual:** Open browser to the liquid glass interface running on dark visionOS aesthetic. Soft neon orbs glowing in the background. Top status pill displays `System Ready • 5 Agents Idle`.
- **Narration:**
  > "Traditional LLMs produce shallow, generic research reports prone to hallucinations and missing counter-evidence.
  > Today, I'm presenting the **Multi-Agent Research & Report Factory**—an autonomous research plant powered by 5 specialized agents collaborating across an asynchronous typed message bus.
  > Rather than a naive linear chain, our platform implements **evaluator-optimizer feedback loops** where an Epistemic Critic actively audits evidence sufficiency, rejects weak claims, and commands targeted re-querying before report synthesis."

---

## Part 2: Workflow Trigger & Live Pipeline (0:40 – 1:30)
- **Visual:** Select Preset `"Commercial Viability of Solid-State Batteries by 2030"`. Toggle **Red Team Mode** on. Click the primary gradient **Launch Research** button.
- **Narration:**
  > "Let's launch an executive research run on *'Commercial Viability of Solid-State Batteries by 2030'*, activating Red Team stress testing.
  > Immediately, Station 01—our **Strategic Planner**—deconstructs the inquiry into MECE sub-questions and working hypotheses.
  > Station 02—the **Scout Searcher**—probes live academic and institutional endpoints.
  > Station 03—the **Deep Reader**—parses and extracts granular quantitative facts into shared memory."

---

## Part 3: The Non-Linear Critic Feedback Loop (1:30 – 2:15)
- **Visual:** Camera zooms into Station 04 (Critic). Watch the epistemic audit calculate score (74/100). The animated curved amber feedback loop line fires back from Critic to Scout.
- **Narration:**
  > "Notice what happens at Station 04: The **Epistemic Critic** evaluates the evidence bank and identifies a critical knowledge gap: *'Need granular 2025-2026 economic benchmarks on operational TCO versus legacy infrastructure.'*
  > Rather than proceeding blindly, the Critic **rejects the evidence sufficiency**, emits a `revision_request`, and forces a non-linear feedback loop.
  > The Scout automatically dispatches refined search vectors, the Reader extracts new evidence, and the Planner updates active hypotheses in shared memory. Notice the Feedback Loops stat card increment to Loop #1."

---

## Part 4: Verified Whitepaper Deliverable & Trace Bus (2:15 – 2:50)
- **Visual:** Workflow transitions to Station 05 (Executive Synthesizer). The final report renders with interactive footnote citations `[^1]`, executive risk matrix, and bibliography. Then click to open the **Trace Bus & Memory Inspector**.
- **Narration:**
  > "Once the Critic signs off on the evidence, Station 05 synthesizes an exhaustive, C-suite whitepaper where every single comparative benchmark maps directly to an audited footnote reference.
  > Evaluators can switch to the **Trace Bus** view to inspect every inter-agent message, tool call telemetry in milliseconds, shared run memory transitions, and export the entire run as machine-readable JSON or Markdown."

---

## Part 5: Conclusion & Reproducibility (2:50 – 3:00)
- **Visual:** Show terminal running `npm test` with 10 passing Vitest tests across 6 suites, and Next.js clean production build.
- **Narration:**
  > "The entire system is open-source, fully test-covered with Vitest, and deployable with zero friction. Thank you!"
